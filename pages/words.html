Pronunciation Tips

  • All words end in 'e' (often silent)
  • No difference between p/b, t/d, k/g
  • 'w' after consonants = round your lips
  • Stress usually on first vowel after consonant
  • For words with 'rr': Roll or tap your tongue
  • For words with 'kng': Blend k+ng in one sound (like a half-sneeze)
  • For words with 'lh', 'nh', 'th': Tongue touches back of front teeth
  • For words with 'w' (rounded): Round your lips like saying "window"
  • For words starting with 'a': This 'a' might be silent - both ways are correct
  • For words ending in 'e': Usually silent, especially before vowels
